The Board, Translated.

Eleven pedals, one HX Stomp XL, two DAWs, and an honest accounting of what survives the trip. Mapping the physical board (plus a Microcosm curveball) into Helix models, plugin substitutes, and mono-friendly monitoring for the Peavey XR600F / Harbinger M60 and the single KRK Rokit 8.

Guitar Mono Out
  • Eight of eleven pedals translate cleanly. DM-2w, DC-2w, Archer, Rattler, Holy Grail, the Lehle, the Peterson's stage duties, and the Enigmatic's ODS voice all have strong native homes in firmware 3.80.
  • MOOD and Microcosm do not translate, and neither has an official plugin. They stay hardware. The best digital stand-in is a dedicated granular plugin (Arturia Efx FRAGMENTS, $99) plus what Live 12 Suite and Logic already ship.
  • Both of your rigs are mono. The Dimension C's whole identity is a stereo crossmix, so it collapses to a mild thicken. Set Global EQ low cut around 80 to 100 Hz, high cut 8 to 10 kHz, and save true stereo judgment for headphones or the DAW.

Board to HX Mapping

11 pedals / firmware 3.80 / 8-block ceiling

The Stomp XL runs the full Helix core model set: 8 blocks per preset, 4 snapshots, 8 footswitches, a stereo FX loop, and Global EQ with parametric bands plus variable low and high cuts. Current firmware is 3.80 (released 19 Nov 2024, the "19th free update" per Line 6's Eric Klein). Nothing newer has shipped for the HX line, so 3.80 is the ceiling for this whole document.

04

Plugin Substitutes

Logic Pro + Ableton Live 12 Suite / VST3 + AU
The headlineAs of mid-2026 there is no official Chase Bliss MOOD plugin (their Goodhertz collaboration went the other way: Lossy became a pedal) and no official Hologram Microcosm plugin. Both boxes are hardware-only and genuinely unique. Below is the best digital adjacency money can and cannot buy.

MOOD + Microcosm territory

Buy first

Arturia Efx FRAGMENTS

VST/VST3/AU/AAX/NKS · $99 (often on sale)

Real-time granular processor: stutters, grainy stereo ambience, texture and rhythmic modes. The name that keeps coming up as the guitar-into-granular Microcosm alternative, and it earns it.

If Fragments isn't deep enough

Output Portal

VST3/AU/AAX · ~$129

Playable grain engine, excellent for MOOD-style clouds. Pair with Valhalla Supermassive (free) for the wash underneath.

Unfiltered Audio Silo

VST3/AU/AAX · ~$99

Granular sample-and-scatter delay; strong glitch textures.

Audio Damage Other Desert Cities

VST3/AU/AAX · ~$79

Granular delay with reverse and grain modes for Microcosm-ish delay glitch.

Free path: Ableton Live 12 Suite
  • Granulator III (Suite bundle, download from Ableton.com): 3 modes incl. Cloud, real-time capture, MPE. Your closest native granular engine.
  • Beat Repeat (stutter), Echo (dub delay), Hybrid Reverb, Roar (color), Looper.
  • Chain: Looper → Granulator III → Hybrid Reverb ≈ the Microcosm capture-and-scatter workflow.
Free path: Logic Pro 12.3
  • Sample Alchemy / Alchemy granular engines (12.3 added tempo-locked Sync and formant shifting).
  • Delay Designer (26 taps), Space Designer, Sculpture, Beat Breaker (slice/glitch), ChromaGlow.
  • Chain: record a phrase → Sample Alchemy granular → Space Designer.

Monitoring, Mono, and EQ

Peavey XR600F + Harbinger M60 / single KRK Rokit 8

Two mono, full-range-ish outputs; neither is a flat FRFR wedge. Treat the HX output as a finished front-of-house signal (amp + cab always on) and tame each rig with Global EQ.

Peavey / HarbingerLoCut 90-100 Hz (a 10" gains nothing below), HiCut 7.5-8.5 kHz (horn fizz). If harsh, pull 2-3 dB at 2.5-4 kHz parametric. Conservative channel gain on the XR600F; the HX runs near line level.
KRK Rokit 8LoCut 80 Hz, HiCut 9-10 kHz (it's honest up top, keep the air). Flatten parametric bands unless the room rings. The 8" woofer will fart out on sub-heavy IRs at volume.
Mono summingFold L/R to the mono jack; audition every stereo block for phase weirdness. If a stereo effect thins in mono, switch that block to its Mono variant.
HeadroomChannel Volume + block Levels so the output meter never clips. Re-check the whole card whenever you swap rigs.
07

Verdicts + Next Moves

where the digital rig ends and the boxes stay unique

Translates

  • DM-2w → Bucket Brigade / Adriatic: same circuit lineage
  • DC-2w → Dimension: the actual SDD-320
  • Archer → Minotaur; Rattler → Vermin
  • Holy Grail spring + hall
  • Enigmatic's ODS character via Litigator
  • Lehle + Peterson stage duties

Approximate

  • Litigator is one ODS voice, not the Enigmatic's four eras (UAD native is the 1:1)
  • Flerb needs the Plate→Flanger rebuild
  • DC-2 in mono loses its identity
  • RC-5 minus drums, memories, storage
  • Rattler's boutique op-amp warmth, close not equal

Doesn't

  • MOOD: no always-listening micro-looper, no stretch, no plugin exists
  • Microcosm: no mosaic/arp/sequence engine anywhere in software, official or otherwise
  • Keep both. Run them in the FX loop. That's the answer.

Move list

  1. Build Preset 01 tonight. Litigator + D-style cab, Minotaur and Vermin in front, Dimension / Bucket Brigade / 63 Spring behind. Six pedals in one patch. If DSP balks, single stock cab instead of dual IR.
  2. MOOD and Microcosm stay physical, in the FX loop. For in-the-box work: Arturia Efx FRAGMENTS ($99) first, Output Portal only if Fragments runs shallow. If you reach for MOOD's freeze/stretch more than its texture, no plugin will satisfy; the pedal stays.
  3. UAD Enigmatic '82 native ($99) for tracking: the exact engine, all four D-style eras, no UA hardware needed. Litigator live, UAD in the studio.
  4. Arturia Chorus DIMENSION-D + Delay MEMORY-BRIGADE on the next sale: more accurate than the HX blocks for mixing, and DIMENSION-D gives you the real stereo crossmix your monitors can't.
  5. Peterson on the bench, HX tuner on stage. RC-5 survives only if you use its drums or song memories.
  6. Set the Global EQ card before judging any tone. It's the single biggest quality jump on non-FRFR mono monitoring, and it needs re-checking every rig swap.

Caveats

  • Firmware: everything here reflects HX/Helix 3.80 (19 Nov 2024), current for the whole HX line. A future update could change model lists.
  • Lehle: Mono Volume / Mono Volume 90 are active VCA pedals, not passive. If yours is truly passive it is another brand or the Dual Expression. Mapping unchanged either way.
  • JAM ID: two knobs plus tone, LM308 lineage, custom bus art = Rattler. If it's actually a Fuzz Phrase or TubeDreamer, swap to Fuzz Face or Tube Screamer models.
  • Holy Grail version: settings assume Nano/original (Spring/Hall/Flerb). Neo owners use Plate directly.
  • Pricing: list/typical mid-2026, moves constantly. UAD intro pricing expired 25 May 2026. MOOD MKII hardware: $399 US, recently $359 direct.
  • DSP: Litigator is hungry; some builds hit the wall before 8 blocks. Stock cab over dual IR is the standard escape hatch.
  • Mono reality: every stereo effect here is compromised on your current monitoring. Judge stereo work on headphones or in the DAW, full stop.
