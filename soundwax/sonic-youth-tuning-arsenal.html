01 / Origin

Both Thurston Moore and Lee Ranaldo came out of Glenn Branca's massed-guitar ensembles in early-eighties New York, where a dozen guitars were tuned to specific pitches from the harmonic series and played as one enormous instrument. Ranaldo has described Branca deciding to tune his guitars like a choir. Ranaldo, Uncut

What they carried out of that room was not a theory. It was a permission slip. The band's early instruments were pawnshop junk, and Ranaldo has said the guitars would not hold a normal tuning, so they started using them more as noise-making devices. Ranaldo, Elektron, 2018

The turning point came right after Confusion Is Sex. Moore stumbled into F♯F♯F♯F♯EB and called it the first real tuning that was sort of notated. Moore, Guitarist, 2015 Songs came out of it immediately, and the method was set: find a tuning, write in it, dedicate a guitar to it, never take it apart.

Tune the guitar a new way and you were a beginner all over again. Ranaldo's argument was that it threw out everything he already knew about the fretboard, which was the point.Lee Ranaldo, LEO Weekly
Kim Gordon on picking up a guitar in a band already built on strange tunings: I almost felt like I had to work against learning how to play.Kim Gordon, NPR, 2015
The grammar

Two facts explain almost every tuning here

02 / Key

Unison and octave pairs. Two strings tuned to the same pitch never land on exactly the same frequency. The tiny difference produces amplitude beating, which your ear reads as a natural chorus. Stack four of them and you get the liquid, ringing, slightly seasick drone that is the band's fingerprint. On the strips below, unison pairs are bracketed underneath.

A narrow range. Standard EADGBE spans two octaves. Joe Gore, writing on the band's setups, noted that in their tunings the distance between the highest and lowest strings was usually an octave or less. Joe Gore, Premier Guitar That compression is why both guitarists could play melodies by barring straight across, and why the two guitars sit on top of each other without either one disappearing.

Notation

Six letters, lowest string first. GGDDGG means strings six and five are both G, four and three are both D, two and one are both G.

Octaves are the soft spot

Almost no source writes octaves down. The octave placements here are playable reconstructions, chosen so the tension math works. Where a tuning has a well-known alternate voicing it is flagged in the panel.

Confidence

Confirmed means liner notes, an interview, or the techs' own gear list. Reported means a single solid secondary source. Transcribed means fans worked it out by ear. Trust accordingly.

Shapes, not tabs

The fingerings are positions that produce the character of the tuning and, where noted, the shape a specific part sits in. They are not note-for-note transcriptions.

05 / Two hands

They almost never played the same tuning at the same time. Moore described it plainly: Lee would either double him in unison or invent something complementary, and it was never discussed.

Thurston
Rhythm plate · magenta
  • Stacks one note. F♯F♯F♯F♯EB, GGDDE♭E♭, DDAF♯AD. Four strings on a single pitch and two left over for melody.
  • Self-taught approach, percussive right hand, drumsticks and screwdrivers jammed under the strings before he ever wrote a tuning down.
  • Chooses tunings that let him hammer a barre and get a wall. The tuning does the harmony so the hand can do the violence.
  • Late period he settles into CGDGCD, borrowed off Pavement and bent to his own use. It runs from Murray Street to The Eternal.
  • His clean signature is a low root plus a fifth plus a suspended second. Never quite major, never quite minor.
Lee
Resonance plate · cyan
  • Came to open tunings through Joni Mitchell, John Fahey and Reverend Gary Davis before he ever met Branca. The folk lineage is audible.
  • Builds tunings that ring. GGDDGG, AAEEAA, GGCGCD. Stacked fifths and fourths that sustain and bloom instead of crunching.
  • Treats the retune as a compositional constraint. Being a beginner again was the feature, not a side effect.
  • Sits above Thurston in register more often than not, so his part reads as melody even when it is just open strings ringing.
  • From Murray Street on, GGDGGA is his home base, a modification of the GGDAGA he used across A Thousand Leaves.

07 / Setup

Three quarters of an inch of scale length changes everything. The same gauge at the same pitch carries about 11% more tension on 25.5" than on 24.75", because tension scales with the square of the string length. That one number decides which guitar gets which tuning.

Sire S-series Strat
25.5" scaleFloating vibratoBone or synthetic nut

Give it the slack tunings. CGDGCD, EGDGED, DDDDAA, DADABD. Anything with a low C or a low D benefits from the extra length, which keeps the fat strings from turning to spaghetti and holds the intonation together.

  • Block the trem. A hardwood block in the cavity plus tightened claw screws. The moment you change overall tension by more than a couple of pounds a floating bridge re-levels itself and every string goes out at once. Blocked, you set relief and intonation once and it stays.
  • Rebalance the claw before you block it, so the bridge sits flat at the new total tension. Check the totals in the lab above against your current set.
  • Nut slots. Anything over .052 on the sixth will bind in a stock slot. Have it filed. A binding slot is the single most common cause of "it will not stay in tune in this tuning."
  • Re-intonate every time. On unison pairs this is not optional. Two strings on the same pitch that are intonated differently do not shimmer, they sour.
Epiphone LP Special, 2017
24.75" scaleFixed bridgeSet neck

Give it the tuned-up tunings. F♯F♯F♯F♯EB, AAEEAA, EEBBEF♯. That 11% tension discount is exactly what you want when strings are going above standard pitch, and the fixed bridge does not care what the total load is.

  • Check the bridge type. A lightning-bar wraparound only intonates as a whole unit, which is a real problem for unison pairs. If yours is a wraparound, a compensated replacement bar or a tune-o-matic conversion is the highest-value mod you can make for this project.
  • Watch the post lean. Wraparounds and older tune-o-matics tilt forward under heavy low strings. Check it after a week in any tuning with a low C or B.
  • Tuning up is the risk. Drop one gauge for roughly every two or three semitones you raise a string. Never take a stock plain string more than three semitones up.
  • Lubricate the nut slots and saddles with graphite. Breakage on up-tuned strings almost always happens at a binding point, not in the middle of the string.

Month one · Sire

Everything is tuned down from standard, so it is close to a normal 11 set and costs you almost no setup work. Learn Rain on Tin, Stones and The Empty Page. This tuning is the highest reward-to-hassle ratio in the whole catalogue and it makes you write differently within a week.

Month two · Epiphone
F♯F♯F♯F♯EB
.038w .038w .018 .018 .010 .014

The iconic one, and the one that will teach you the most about tension. Kool Thing, 100%, Star Power. Two of the strings go well above standard pitch, so build it light on top and expect to change the first string more often than you would like. If the B keeps snapping, voice it an octave lower and use a .014.

Month three · both
DDDDAA + CGDGCD
.056 .046 .036 .026 .017 .013

Lee's tuning on one guitar, Thurston's on the other, and now you can hear what this band actually was. Record a loop in one and play the other over it. Everything in the interlock section above is built to be practised exactly this way.

Sources and caveats

08 / Notes

The tuning lore is not gospel. Chris Lawrence's tuning tutorial on sonicyouth.com is the closest thing to a canonical list, and it was assembled from set lists, headstock photographs and interviews over many years. Lawrence flags his own uncertainties, including that printed liner-note tunings may not be accurate.

Octaves are the weakest link. Almost no source records them. Several entries in the fan lists say outright that the compiler is unsure whether two strings are in unison or an octave apart. Every octave placement in this document is a reconstruction chosen so the string tension lands in a playable range, cross-checked against the band's own published gauge charts.

Multi-guitar songs use multiple simultaneous tunings. Where you see one tuning attached to a song title, that is usually a simplification of a two-guitar arrangement.

The theft numbers vary. Contemporary reporting in 1999 described roughly two dozen customised guitars taken. The fan-run gear guide later catalogued a larger set. Both agree the truck turned up empty in downtown Los Angeles a few days later.
