The Tuning Arsenal — Sonic Youth, EVOL to The Eternal

Twenty-seven tunings with tappable strings, fretboard diagrams, plucked audio and a live tension calculator. Bring a guitar you can afford to detune.

 Instrument 02

The Savernake Gazette: The Making of The King of Limbs

Two buildings, twenty months, no clock, and a two-week turntable experiment that ate the record.

 Report 03

THE CURE: 42 Years In Between

One band, two records, and the studio experience measured across four decades. Pornography cut at RAK in about three weeks in 1982; Songs of a Lost World assembled over five years into the 2020s. What changed in how they worked, and what the room decided either way.

 Report 04

No Robot Unturned: Grandaddy’s The Sophtware Slump

Skateboarders from a town people drive through, a converted warehouse called Little Portugal, eleven transmissions, and acclaim without sales.

 Report 05

Remain in Light: A Listener’s Reference

Talking Heads at Compass Point and Sigma Sound, from the Fela blueprint to the credit dispute that broke the band. Track by track, board to sleeve.

Signal Path — Studio Operations Manual

Four projects, one room, and a tappable patch bay that traces what feeds what. Pick tonight’s job, get the shortest route to it, and see what is going to bite you.

 Ops manual 02

SPD-SX Pro: A Working Guide

A used pad on firmware 2.0, treated as an instrument. Wave organisation, the real 32-voice ceiling, and rig integration. No menu hand-holding.

 Log 03

SPD-SX Pro: Kit Builder Guide, Ver. 2.00

One pad runs two loops in sync, the next kills it and starts another, and hitting harder brings in a third. Layers, mute groups, and the sequencer that is not one.

 Instrument 04

Where to get them, what the law actually says, how to make your own, and how to blend until the fingerprints are yours.

 Field manual 05

The Night Practice Book

Minor keys, jazz voicings, and the why behind chords you already play by ear. Am(maj7) is most of the book. Fingerings for both hands, six-week plan at the back.

 Instrument 06

The Engine Room — Night Practice Book, Vol. II

The physics under the harmony. Intervals as ratios, fifths as structure, sevenths as vocabulary, three pedals, and what changes on Rhodes, Wurli and synth.
